# What Is Foundation and How to Build It?

> SMART means Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, and Time-sensitive.

A strong foundation is built from the things that improve your ability to live, learn, work, and grow.

```
Foundation
├── Requirements
│   ├── Focus
│   ├── Persistence
│   ├── Self-management
│   └── Optimistic mindset
├── A Fit Body
├── A Calm Mind
├── Relationships
├── Competency
├── Personal Core Values
├── Fix Your Past
├── Setup Your Future
└── Quotes
```

---

## 1. Requirements

### 1.1 Focus

**EFFECTIVENESS = TIME × ENERGY × FOCUS**

When you focus, you can save a lot of time.

Key principles:

- Focus on one thing at a time.
- Remove distractions.
- Keep continuous working hours.
- Avoid switching tasks frequently.

---

### 1.2 Persistence, Consistency, and Patience

- Persistence (Kiên trì)
- Consistency (Nhất quán)
- Patience

Long-term results come from repeating the right actions.

---

### 1.3 Reflection and Planning

Reflect and plan for the new day.

Process:

1. Absorb
2. Respond
3. Reflect
4. Act
5. Share

---

### 1.4 Self-management

#### Do what you said you will do

Build trust with yourself by keeping promises.

#### Self-control

Control your emotions, habits, and reactions.

#### Calmness

Stay calm when facing problems.

---

### 1.5 Observation and Evaluation

Find the root of the problem.

Principle:

> Address the root cause, don't only react to the behavior.

Ask deeper questions:

- Why did this happen?
- What caused this?
- What can I improve?

---

### 1.6 Ability to Learn New Skills

Develop the ability to learn continuously.

Learning itself is a skill.

---

### 1.7 Optimistic Mindset

Everything has two sides. Find the positive side.

The first thing to do is realize:

> You can.

Examples:

- You can learn to be happy.
- You can improve yourself.
- You can build new skills.
