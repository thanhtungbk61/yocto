    What Is Foundation and How to Build It?¶

    SMART means Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, and Time-sensitive.

    A strong foundation is built from the things that improve your ability to live, learn, work, and grow.

    1. Requirements¶

    1.1 Focus¶

    EFFECTIVENESS = TIME × ENERGY × FOCUS

    When you focus, you can save a lot of time.

    Key principles:

    • Focus on one thing at a time.
    • Remove distractions.
    • Keep continuous working hours.
    • Avoid switching tasks frequently.

    1.2 Persistence, Consistency, and Patience¶

    • Persistence (Kiên trì)
    • Consistency (Nhất quán)
    • Patience

    Long-term results come from repeating the right actions.

    1.3 Reflection and Planning¶

    Reflect and plan for the new day.

    Process:

    1. Absorb
    2. Respond
    3. Reflect
    4. Act
    5. Share

    1.4 Self-management¶

    Do what you said you will do¶

    Build trust with yourself by keeping promises.

    Self-control¶

    Control your emotions, habits, and reactions.

    Calmness¶

    Stay calm when facing problems.

    1.5 Observation and Evaluation¶

    Find the root of the problem.

    Principle:

    Address the root cause, don't only react to the behavior.

    Ask deeper questions:

    • Why did this happen?
    • What caused this?
    • What can I improve?

    1.6 Ability to Learn New Skills¶

    Develop the ability to learn continuously.

    Learning itself is a skill.

    1.7 Optimistic Mindset¶

    Everything has two sides. Find the positive side.

    The first thing to do is realize:

    You can.

    Examples:

    • You can learn to be happy.
    • You can improve yourself.
    • You can build new skills.

    So he just does not care what’s in the way. Nothing gets him down. He’s always laughing or smiling. He’s always moving through it. And this is the kind of guy that a hundred years ago you would’ve said, “Oh, he’s the most courageous. Go charge that machine gun nest.”

    => He’s always laughing or smiling. optimism

    2. A Fit Body (Physical Part)¶

    A healthy body supports everything else.

    Exercise Plan¶

    • Football:
      • Tuesday
      • Thursday
      • Saturday
    • Workout:
      • 19:15 → 20:00

    Goals:

    • Increase energy.
    • Maintain health.
    • Build discipline.

    3. A Calm Mind (Mental Part)¶

    A calm mind helps you make better decisions.

    Questions:

    • Why do people become stressed?
    • What creates anxiety?
    • How can we control our thoughts?

    4.2 Friends¶

    7. The Most Important Thing¶

    Always identify the most important thing.

    Focus your time and energy on what creates the biggest impact.

    9. Setup Your Future¶

    9.1 Upwind¶

    Stay in a direction that helps you grow.

    9.2 Purpose¶

    10. Other Thoughts¶

    10.1 Environment and Personal Growth¶

    Live in New York City once, but leave before it makes you hard; Live in Northern California once, but leave before it makes you soft.

    Experience different ways of living, but don't let any single environment completely define who you are.

    Learn from each place, then move on before its strengths become your weaknesses.
